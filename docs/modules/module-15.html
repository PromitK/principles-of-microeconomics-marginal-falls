The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

Main Street has many businesses, but they are not identical. Moonlit Mocha can retain some customers after raising its price because people value its coffee, atmosphere, or location. Differentiation introduces market power alongside competition and entry.

A question to take into class

How can Moonlit Mocha have some market power while still facing many competitors?
