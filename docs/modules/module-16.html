The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

Two shuttle operators choose fares, and each operator's profit depends on its rival's decision. The boardroom introduces strategic interdependence: a sensible choice cannot be assessed without asking what the other firm will do.

A question to take into class

Why can the firms reach an outcome that neither would choose if they could jointly commit to fares?

The economic ideas

Concepts to follow

  • Oligopoly and strategic interaction
  • Payoff matrices and best responses
  • Nash equilibrium
  • Prisoner's dilemma and cooperation
