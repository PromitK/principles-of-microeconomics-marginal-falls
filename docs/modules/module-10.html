The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

The town has a market price, but who gains from exchange? The Berry Vault introduces reservation values and surplus. Price controls and taxes then show how policies can change the distribution of gains and the number of mutually beneficial trades.

A question to take into class

How can a policy intended to help one group change both who gains and how much total surplus the town creates?

The economic ideas

Concepts to follow

  • Efficiency and deadweight loss
  • Price ceilings and price floors
  • Tax incidence
