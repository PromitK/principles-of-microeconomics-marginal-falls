The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

Marginal Falls has ambitious plans for parks, homes, roads, and festivals, but limited land, time, and money. The founding decisions introduce the economic problem that runs through the course: choosing among desirable uses of scarce resources.

A question to take into class

When the mayor changes a rule, why might citizens respond differently from what the mayor intended?
