The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

The bakery has chosen its output, but how far can it expand when pastry prices rise? Capacity, the availability of inputs, and time determine how responsive sellers can be. The slide deck introduces this module as Seller Sensitivity.

A question to take into class

Why might the bakery respond little to a price increase tonight but much more over the next year?

The economic ideas

Concepts to follow
