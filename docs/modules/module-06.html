The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

Founders' Night brings a large order for bread, pastries, cakes, and snack boxes. Behind the counter, the bakery must combine workers and equipment. The production process connects additional workers to output and then to the cost of serving the town.

A question to take into class

Why can an additional worker add less output when the bakery's equipment stays fixed?
