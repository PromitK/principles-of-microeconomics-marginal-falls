The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

A price increase does not affect every purchase equally. Sorting the town's goods by buyer responsiveness makes elasticity concrete and connects substitutes, time, and necessity to the effect of a price change on revenue.

A question to take into class

Why might buyers react strongly to a higher coffee price but only slightly to a higher price for an urgent purchase?
