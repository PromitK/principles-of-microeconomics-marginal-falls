The Farmers Market

Perfect competition

View lecture slides

37 slides · Hubbard & O'Brien, chapter 12.

The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

Many stalls sell comparable products, and no single stall controls the market price. The farmers market returns to output and profit decisions in a competitive setting and examines how entry and exit change the longer-run outcome.

A question to take into class

Why does a competitive stall take the market price as given even though it chooses its own output?

The economic ideas

Concepts to follow

  • Price taking
  • Firm and market demand
  • Short-run output decisions
  • Entry, exit, and long-run equilibrium
