The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

The bakery receives another order and considers hiring. Workers are valuable because their production can be sold, so labor demand is derived from demand for the bakery's output. The hiring hall connects product markets to input prices and wages.

A question to take into class

How does greater demand for pastries affect the value of an additional bakery worker?
