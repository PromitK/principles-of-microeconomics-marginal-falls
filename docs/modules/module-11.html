The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

A smoke complaint introduces a resident who is affected by a transaction without being a buyer or seller. The town must now distinguish private incentives from social costs and benefits, and ask how goods used collectively will be provided.

A question to take into class

Can an exchange benefit its buyer and seller while imposing a cost on someone else in Marginal Falls?

The economic ideas

Concepts to follow

  • Positive and negative externalities
  • Private and social costs and benefits
  • Public goods
  • Free riding and policy responses
