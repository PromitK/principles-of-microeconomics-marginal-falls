The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

The mayor steps back from announcing prices. Buyers arrive with demand plans and sellers arrive with supply plans. The town square shows how prices coordinate those plans and how shortages and surpluses put pressure on the market outcome.

A question to take into class

What happens when the posted price makes buyers want more pastries than sellers are willing to provide?

The economic ideas

Concepts to follow

  • Equilibrium price and quantity
  • Shortages and surpluses
  • Supply and demand shifts
  • Prices as a coordination mechanism
