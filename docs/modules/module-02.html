The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

The port opens, and the town must decide what to make and what to obtain through exchange. Comparing opportunity costs shows why a town can gain from trade even when its trading partner is more productive at making every good.

A question to take into class

If another town can produce both goods using fewer resources, can Marginal Falls still benefit from trading with it?
