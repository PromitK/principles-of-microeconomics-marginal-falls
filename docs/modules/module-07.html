The Shopkeeper's Offer

Profit maximization and the supply curve

View lecture slides

24 slides · Hubbard & O'Brien, chapter 3 (Part B).

The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

The mayor announces a pastry price. Each seller must decide how much to produce at that price. Comparing the revenue and cost of additional output reveals how a firm's production choices become a supply curve.

A question to take into class

At a given market price, what tells the bakery whether producing one more pastry raises profit?
