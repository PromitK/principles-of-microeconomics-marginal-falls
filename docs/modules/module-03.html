The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

Citizens want coffee, pastries, ferry rides, and answers to a mystery. The investigation activities turn a limited budget into a choice between competing uses of resources, connecting the value of one more purchase to the best affordable bundle.

A question to take into class

When is buying one more clue worthwhile, and how should a fixed investigation budget be divided?
