The Corporate District

Monopoly and market power

The story

Where this fits in Marginal Falls

River-Gate is the only seller of bridge passes for entry to Founders' Night. There is no nearby alternative. The corporate district uses this bottleneck to connect barriers to entry with market power, a downward-sloping demand curve, and the monopoly's output decision.

A question to take into class

Why must River-Gate consider the effect on its existing sales when deciding whether to sell another bridge pass?
